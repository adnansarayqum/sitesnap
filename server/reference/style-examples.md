# Style examples

Register and sentence construction the firm has settled on. These are the
surveyor's own approved wordings — use them to hear the voice, not as
templates to fill in (see corrections.md, rule 7).

## Observation → cause (voice note, bathroom)

> Damp and mould was observed to areas of the wall adjacent to the bathtub at
> the time of inspection. On the balance of probabilities the mould is
> considered to be condensation-related: moisture readings of 4.5% were
> recorded to the affected area, and the extractor fan was found to be
> non-operational and under-powered when tested by paper test and anemometer.
> Staining and blistering of the paint finish was also observed to the wall
> opposite the bathtub, which is consistent with an underlying leak; on the
> balance of probabilities two causes are present.

What it does: pins every observation to the visit; records readings; names
both causes with the evidence for each; uses the standard of proof once per
causal step.

## Remedial — scope set honestly (ceiling cracking, textured coating)

The surveyor's final wording after rejecting two AI drafts (one under-scoped
to a patch, one over-scoped to "remove the coating across the entire ceiling"):

> Subject to the asbestos results, the defective ceiling finish should be
> appropriately prepared and made good, including treatment of the cracking,
> associated waste disposal and sufficient redecoration to provide a
> consistent finish throughout the affected ceiling area.

What it does: conditions the works on the sample; covers preparation,
treatment, disposal and decoration in one sentence; bounds the scope with
"the affected ceiling area" rather than "the entire ceiling".

## Remedial — symptom plus finish (window reveals)

> The mould-affected window reveals should be cleaned and treated with an
> appropriate fungicidal mould treatment. Defective decorative finishes should
> be prepared, sealed with an appropriate stain-blocking treatment and
> redecorated using a moisture-resistant paint system.

## Investigation first (asbestos)

> The ceiling should be inspected and tested for the presence of asbestos
> before any remedial works take place.
