Privacy Policy

Last updated 8 September 2026 · Applies to the SiteSnap app and its associated web service.

SiteSnap is a property-inspection app used by surveying firms to take, organise and file inspection photos, voice notes and disrepair findings. This page explains what the app collects, why, and where it goes.

What the app collects

  • Inspection content you create — photos, voice notes, room notes, and (where AI drafting is switched on) findings drafted from that content. This is the core of what the app is for.
  • Case details — the property address, postcode, and inspection status you enter.
  • Account information, only if your firm uses the accounts/sign-in version of the app — your email address, and if you sign in with Microsoft or Google, the basic profile information those services share (name, email). The app never sees your Microsoft/Google password.
  • Device and session information needed to keep you signed in and the app working — a session cookie, and basic technical details (app version, device type) if error monitoring is switched on for your deployment.

Where it's stored

By default, everything you capture is stored on your own device first. Depending on how your firm has configured SiteSnap:

  • Case records may sync to a private database operated for your firm ("accounts mode"), so your firm's cases are visible to your firm's team and nowhere else.
  • Photos and case files may be uploaded to a cloud drive (OneDrive or Google Drive) that you or your firm connects — SiteSnap only ever reaches the dedicated folder it creates for itself in that drive, never anything else in your account.

Who else sees it

SiteSnap doesn't sell or share your data with advertisers. Depending on which features your deployment has switched on, content may be sent to:

  • Anthropic (the maker of Claude), if AI drafting is enabled, to draft findings from your photos and notes.
  • OpenAI, if voice-note transcription is enabled, to transcribe voice notes to text.
  • Microsoft or Google, only for the cloud folder you've explicitly connected.
  • Sentry, if error monitoring is enabled for your deployment, to report crashes — configured to exclude request content, cookies, and AI prompts/ responses; it only ever receives the error itself and enough context (which screen, which route) to debug it.

Your choices

  • A case can be deleted from within the app at any time, which removes it from your firm's register. Anything already uploaded to a connected OneDrive/Google Drive isn't removed automatically — that copy lives in your own cloud storage and is managed there.
  • Voice-note transcription and AI drafting are both optional, set per deployment — a firm can use SiteSnap purely for photo capture and organising, with nothing sent to a language model.
